### From an idea to a working system

At **Rapple AI**, I focus on lead generation and the infrastructure behind it: CRM, funnels, AI voice agents, qualification, and follow-up for US local service businesses.

Here on GitHub, I share the tools and resources I build along the way — from reusable automation workflows to a local dictation app and an AI business knowledge starter kit.
